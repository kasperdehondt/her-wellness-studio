
## Stephanie — Wellness & Art Portfolio

A serene, earthy one-page showcase with a split-screen hero and smooth scroll sections.

### Design Direction
- **Palette**: Terracotta (#c4654a) & sage (#87a878) with warm neutrals (#e8a87c, #faf8f5)
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond for elegant headings, Karla for clean body text
- **Layout**: Split-screen hero, flowing into full-width content sections
- **Vibe**: Organic, grounded, luxurious wellness — generous whitespace, subtle hover animations

### Sections (single page, smooth scroll)

1. **Hero (Split Screen)** — Left: large heading "Stephanie" with tagline "Movement · Healing · Art", a subtle CTA button. Right: warm-toned placeholder image area
2. **About** — Brief bio with a photo, introducing her multi-disciplinary practice
3. **Services** — 4 cards (Yoga, Pilates, Therapy, Art) with icons, short descriptions, and earthy color accents
4. **Gallery / Work** — Masonry-style grid of placeholder images showcasing her work across disciplines
5. **Testimonials** — 2-3 rotating or stacked client quotes
6. **Contact** — Simple form (name, email, message) + location/social links info

### Interactions
- Smooth scroll navigation with a minimal sticky header
- Subtle fade-in animations on scroll
- Hover effects on service cards and gallery items
- Mobile-responsive throughout
